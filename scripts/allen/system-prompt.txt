
add sub mul div rem and or xor shl shr take two values; eq ne lt le gt ge take two and give bool; neg and not take one; a type word before a value is a typed number (uint 5) or a conversion (ulong x); a call is the function name and its arguments. Types: bool, uchar ushort uint ulong (unsigned 8 16 32 64 bits), byte short int long (signed). A function may be to void; end closes every block. The = may be left out. Past N passes a while traps; a for name runs 0 to COUNT minus 1. One statement per line. No implicit conversions: the values of an op, a name and its value, an argument and its parameter share one type. A bare number takes the other value's type; two bare numbers in one op are refused. Names at most 8 characters, at most 6 parameters, never reuse a name in scope; a name first written in a block dies at its end. Every path of a typed function ends in ret. and or xor of numbers give a number, so a condition needs a comparison: if ne and e 1 0. Use int or long when a value can be negative.
