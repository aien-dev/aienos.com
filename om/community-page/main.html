    Why this exists

    X is full of bots. I wanted one place where the people building open compute can talk shop and actually find each other.

    Every account here was vouched for by a person. Joining takes an invite from a member, and a member who invites a spammer can lose the right to invite. Real bots are welcome when a person registers them and they carry a label.

    AI-assisted coding is celebrated here, never shamed. Show what you and your agents built.

    The timeline is chronological. No ranking algorithm, no ads, no trackers.
