COMMUNITY / RULES

Community rules

I built this space for people building with Omega, CUDA++ and open compute. These rules keep it a place where builders talk to builders. They apply to every post, profile, bot and report.

Rules Terms Privacy

Who can join

You must be at least 13 years old, and old enough where you live to agree to these rules on your own. One person, one account.

Every account here was vouched for by a person. You join with an invite link from a member and sign in with a passkey, which lives on your device and needs no password. Linking a GitHub account is optional and adds a Verified GitHub badge.

New accounts start on probation for their first 7 days: 8 posts a day, 1 link and 3 mentions per post, no URL shorteners, and no invites. After that an account is a member: 100 posts a day, 4 links and 8 mentions per post.

Invites are a vouch

A member can invite people once their account is 7 days old: at most 3 open links and 5 new ones a week. A link works once, for 14 days. Each profile shows who invited it, and the invite tree is public.

When you invite someone you vouch for them. If two accounts you invited are suspended for spam or abuse within 30 days, your invites pause until a moderator looks. Within 90 days of a join, the person who sent the invite can revoke that vouch, and moderators can revoke any vouch. A revoked account becomes read-only: it can still sign in, export its data and delete itself. Revoking can also cover everyone that account invited, down the tree. Another member can vouch for a revoked account again, which uses one of their invites.

What is not allowed

  • Illegal content. Nothing that is illegal to publish, and nothing that helps someone break the law against another person.
  • Harassment and hate. No threats, no pile-ons, no attacks on people for who they are. Argue about code and ideas as hard as you like; leave the person alone.
  • Spam and undisclosed automation. No repeated promotion, no engagement bait, no posting by a program from a personal account.
  • Doxxing. No home addresses, private phone numbers, private documents or anything else a person has not made public themselves.
  • Impersonation. Do not pretend to be another person, project or company.

Bots

Bots are welcome when a person stands behind them. A bot must be registered by a signed-in human from Settings, at most 2 per person, and it posts only with its own token. Every bot post carries the label Bot, run by @owner, and the owner answers for what it posts. Aien, my site assistant, is labelled Site assistant and replies when someone mentions it, a few times an hour at most.

Coding with AI is welcome

AI-assisted coding is celebrated here, never shamed. Post what you built with your agents, the prompts that worked and the ones that did not. The only thing I ask is honesty: a person posts from a personal account, and automation says that it is automation.

Reports and moderation

Every post and every profile has a Report item in its menu. Pick a reason (spam, harassment, illegal content, impersonation or something else) and add a note if it helps. The person you report never learns who reported them.

A moderator reads each report and decides. A moderator can hide a post, dismiss a report, change an account's tier, suspend an account, revoke a vouch, restore a branch of the invite tree or lift an invite pause. Hidden posts disappear from timelines and search. A suspended account cannot post. I appoint the moderators, and I review their decisions.

If you think a decision about you was wrong, write to drake@aienos.com and I will look at it myself.

The public moderation log

Every moderator action is listed in the moderation log: what was done, when, by which moderator and the public reason. Account actions say "an account" instead of the handle, so the log does not become a list of names. Private notes between moderators stay private.
