Sponsor on GitHub

For individuals and companies. Monthly or one-time, paid through GitHub.

How to sponsor

  1. Open github.com/sponsors/aien-dev while signed in to GitHub.
  2. Choose a monthly or one-time tier.
  3. Sponsor from your personal account, or from your company's GitHub organization.
Fees, invoices and companies

GitHub charges no fee on sponsorships from personal accounts, so all of it reaches the project. Sponsorships from organization accounts carry a GitHub fee of up to 6%, and an organization can pay by invoice instead of card.
